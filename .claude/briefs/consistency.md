# Feature: consistency (F = consistency) — the user's #2 priority (plus goals, trophies, year in review)
Existing stubs: ConsistencyPage ('/consistency'), GoalsPage ('/goals'), TrophiesPage ('/trophies'), YearInReviewPage ('/year'), widgets `StreakWidget`, `GoalsWidget`, `CalendarWidget`. Server under `/api/consistency`, DTOs in packages/shared/src/features/consistency.ts.

1. **Consistency page**: big `CalendarHeatmap` per year (year switcher; metric: active minutes / distance / activities / effort / Move-ring %); click a day → that day's activities (links) + health summary (steps, rings). Streak cards: current & longest daily streak, weekly streak (≥ 1 activity per week), Move / Exercise / Stand ring streaks (activity_summaries), active days this year. Monthly active-days chart; weekday × hour punchcard; consistency score (share of the last 12 weeks meeting the weekly goal or ≥ N active days) with explanation; activity-ring history (daily ring glyphs for the selected month — Apple's red/green/blue only there) and monthly ring-closure rates.
2. **Goals ('/goals')**: CRUD in your own table (period week/month/year; sport or all; metric distance/time/elevation/activities/active days; target); progress with "on track / behind" projection, history of past periods (hit/miss grid), suggestions from recent averages; defaults when none exist.
3. **Trophies ('/trophies')**: derive step → achievements with earned date + activity: milestones (first activity; 10/25/50/100/250/500/1000 activities; cumulative distance per sport 100/500/1000/5000/10000 km; first 5K/10K/half/marathon; longest run/ride thresholds; cumulative elevation incl. "Everest" 8,848 m; streaks 7/30/100/365 days and 4/12/26/52 weeks; early bird / night owl / weekend warrior; ring streaks) plus monthly challenges ("Run 50/100 km", "Climb 1,000 m", "10 activities", "5 × 5K") with current progress and history. Tiered SVG medallions (bronze/silver/gold); locked ones greyed with progress.
4. **Year in Review ('/year', '/year/:year')**: Wrapped-style sections: totals, by sport, top month, longest/fastest, most active weekday & hour, streaks, new PRs, elevation, hours, health highlights from daily_metrics, comparison with the previous year; screenshot-friendly; year switcher.
5. **Widgets**: `StreakWidget`, `GoalsWidget` (top 2–3), `CalendarWidget` (last ~18 weeks).
Pure, unit-tested functions for streaks (daily/weekly with weekStart, gaps, "today not yet active" keeps the streak), goal projection, achievement rules.
